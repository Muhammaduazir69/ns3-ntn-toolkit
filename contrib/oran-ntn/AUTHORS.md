## Acknowledgements

- The overall O-RAN message flow (E2, A1, O1, O2) follows the O-RAN
  Alliance WG1 reference architecture (v10.0) and the O-RAN
  `oran-module` released by NIST (Lacava et al., 2025 TNSM). Those
  prior works provided the public interface contract for E2AP, but
  this module is an independent C++ implementation.
- O-RAN E2SM-KPM v03.00 and E2SM-RC v01.03 ASN.1 definitions are
  derived from the publicly released O-RAN specification.
- Space-RIC autonomous-mode accounting uses the stressed-feeder
  schema from Shen et al., 2025 TNSM.
